# Sword Tool Documentation
## Node Parameters
The parameters are separated into four sections: Blade, Guard, Grip, and Pommel. Each one has its own unique parameters to help you craft your sword.  

### Blade
Length - Length of the blade.  
Blade Geometry - Choose between a double edged or single edged blade.  
Shape - Select your style for the edge of the blade.  
Width - Adjust the width of the blade.  
Thickness - Adjust the thickness of the centerline of the blade.  
Offset - Left to Right offset of the blade relative to the hilt.  
Curve Profile - Choose a curve profile for the body of the blade.  
Curve Profile Intensity - How much the curve profile affects the blade.  
Flip Curve - Whether or not to flip the direction of the curve.  
Resolution - The amount of segments the blade has from tip to hilt (Measured by length of segment).  
Include Guard in Blade Offset - Offset the length of the blade to fit the face of the guard and remove overlap.  
Material - Set the material of the blade.  

### Guard
Shape - Choose either a block guard or disc guard.  
Thickness - Adjust the thickness of the guard.  
Width - Adjust the width of the guard.  
Depth - Adjust the depth of the guard.  
Material - Change the material of the guard.  

Resolution (Disc Only) - Increase the amount of faces along the outside of the disc guard.  

### Grip
Length - Adjust the length of the grip.  
Width - Adjust the width of the grip.  
Flatness - Adjust the flatness of the grip.  
Resolution - Adjust how segmented the outside of the grip is.  
Include Guard in Grip Offset - Adjust grip location to match face of guard.  
Shade Grip Smooth - Choose whether or not to smooth the grip.  
Material - Change material of the grip.  

### Pommel
Radius - Adjust size of the pommel.  
Resolution - Adjust pommel tri-count.  
Offset - Adjust offset of the pommel relative to the grip.  
Shade Grip Smooth - Choose whether or not to smooth the pommel.  
Material - Change material of the Pommel.  

## Exporting
Make sure when exporting assets to additionally unwrap and pack UVs for use in other programs.
